# car-rental-simulation

A simulation of a car rental company testing different incentive programs

**Context**

Provided iqs the aggregated rental data per hour for "Sharetown" spanning two years. The data set is only comprised of the first 19 days of each month.

**Objective** 

Explore the data, visualize the findings and make recommendations for management based on conclusions drawn from the data analysis. 

**Data**

Dataset: <ins>exploratory_data_analysis.csv</ins>

* datetime: hourly date + timestamp
* season: 1=spring, 2=summer, 3=fall, 4=winter
* holiday: whether the day is considered a holiday
* workingday: whether the day is neither a weekend nor holiday
* weather: 1=Clear, Few clouds, Partly cloudy; 2=Mist + Cloudy, Mist + Few clouds, Mist; 3:Light Snow, Light Rain + Thunderstorm + Scattered clouds, Light Rain + Scattered clouds; 4:Heavy Rain + Ice Pallets + Thunderstorm + Mist, Snow + Fog
* temp: temperature in Celsius
* atemp: “feels like” temperature in Celsius
* humidity: relative humidity
* windspeed: wind speed
* casual: number of non registered user rentals initiated
* registered: number of registered user rentals initiated
* count: number of rentals starting in that hour

Dataset: <ins>rental_data.csv</ins>
* Rental_state: “RED” = car is rented, “GREEN” = car is available / idle
* Start: timestamp of start of rental / idle time
* End: Timestamp of end of rental / idle time
* Revenue: revenue generated by rental in €
* Distance: driven distance in km

Dataset: <ins>charge_probabilities.csv</ins>
* Incentive: amount of free minutes
* Charging_probability: probability that the user will charge the car given the incentive offered

Additional Parameters:

- With each kilometer in a rental, the battery level of the car falls
- If the battery level falls below 20%, the car cannot be rented anymore until the service team arrives and puts it on a charging port.
- Battery consumption during rental: 0.5% per km
- Waiting time until service team puts car on charging pole after battery level fell below 20%: 180 mins
- Cost of service team charging one car: 6 €
- Cost of a free minute given as an incentive to users: 0.29€
